Section 01
Context & Recommended Structure
Why an EOR is the right path for hiring in Brazil, and how Wide Brazil supports it

MEQ Probe is looking to engage a professional based in Brazil without opening a local legal entity. Under Brazilian labor law (CLT), any ongoing, subordinate work relationship must be formalized as an employment contract — informal contractor arrangements carry real reclassification risk. An Employer of Record (EOR) lets MEQ Probe hire compliantly and start immediately, while Wide Brazil holds the local employment relationship, payroll, and statutory obligations.

Recommended Structure

Wide Brazil becomes the legal employer of record in Brazil. Your team member is hired under a CLT employment contract, paid monthly in BRL, and fully compliant with local tax, labor, and social security requirements — while working exclusively under MEQ Probe's direction day to day.

Why EOR

No entity setup, no local payroll infrastructure, and no compliance exposure. Wide Brazil absorbs the administrative and legal burden — MEQ Probe manages the working relationship.
